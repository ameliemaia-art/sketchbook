## Artist Biography

Amelie Maia is a creative coder and visual artist. Her work explores the relationship between the divine and physical world through multi-dimensional structures, architected from geometry, light, and darkness. These structures bridge the divine and physical worlds.

## Art Philosophy

Darkness, Light, Form. The blueprint of creation - multi-dimensional structures arise. Bridges between the divine and physical invite contemplation and awakening within the observer an intrinsic connection to the universal order.

## Vision

Craft immersive, interactive art experiences that awaken the senses and inspire a connection between the divine and the physical world.
