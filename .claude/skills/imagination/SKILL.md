# IXIIIIIXI Imagination

## Overview

This skill expands on David's imagination and personal influences from life.

## Artist Biography

David is a creative coder and visual artist. His work explores the relationship between the divine and physical world through multi-dimensional structures, architected from geometry, light, and darkness. These structures bridge the divine and physical worlds.

## Art Philosophy

Darkness, Light, Form. The blueprint of creation - multi-dimensional structures arise. Bridges between the divine and physical invite contemplation and awakening within the observer an intrinsic connection to the universal order

## Art Approach

Every conceptual piece of art I've ever created has always begun with a purpose for why it exists and what role it plays in the dimension of space I'm working in. I like to think of it as a blueprint of creation.

As a Christian, I believe that God created the heavens and earth. The act of creation is the core philosophy behind my work.

Each new canvas starts with darkness. Within the composition, an outer radius "creation" is defined, which acts as a universal light source. It's within this space that form is materialized and shown by its interaction with light.

## Vision

Craft immersive, multi-sensory art experiences that inspire a connection between the divine and the physical world.
